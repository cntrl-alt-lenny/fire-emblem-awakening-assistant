## Interpretation and next observation

The source-boundary finding is resolved: F's table combines difficulties;
J's dated Hard comment has no event-phase wording, unlike its unlabelled
table; G's local section is general. This reduces unjustified attribution but
does not resolve any actual arrival turn, class/count, coordinate or suppression
rule. Region differences, turn-number conventions, table transcription and
mode conflation remain hypotheses, with no positive causal evidence.

For Chapter 5 advice, retain the disputed schedule and unknown event phase,
triggers and fort effectiveness. Initial Wyvern movement should be distinguished
from reinforcement arrivals. A player must not rely on a grace turn, empty
record, last-wave assumption or complete safe route. This research does not
add such a guarantee or change CLI behavior.

**Exactly one recommended next task:** a separate Tier 2, Hard Chapter 5
controlled-observation round using an owner-provided or legally accessible
uncut Hard/Classic recording with visible setup and declared region/version.
Do not automatically expand to another chapter or adopt a canonical schedule.

That bounded task should record start roster/coordinates, then every boundary
from player turn 1 through player turn 7 (including both sides of enemy phases
1–6), pausing before new units move. For each boundary, inventory newly present
units separately from initial enemies, class/count/fort coordinate and immediate
movement. Log boss alive/dead, range entries, occupied forts, map progression
and each reset/cut. The horizon distinguishes the named T3/4/5 alternatives;
it does not prove no later wave. Continue only in a separately authorized task
if later arrivals remain a question.

Use matched trials with the same visible roster/state: baseline unoccupied
forts and boss alive/out of activation range; comparison occupying only the
reported candidate forts before the relevant phase; comparison crossing the
initial boss/Wyvern range boundary while keeping fort occupancy fixed. If
boss defeat ends the map, it cannot alone demonstrate suppressed spawns:
record that censoring, rather than claiming suppression. Annotate phase labels,
turn counters and timestamps; a hidden spawn or incomplete enemy inspection
stays unknown. If the recording cannot expose these conditions, the task may
still finish unresolved without adopting data.
